概念图不是终点，是素材 // THE PROBLEM

发展商 group 里每天都在丢图。Grand Lobby、Sky Garden、Swimming Pool、Gym，一张比一张漂亮。

然后大部分人怎么用？

直接转发去 status，配一句「Lobby 很美」。
放进 carousel，第三张开始就没人滑了。
想剪 Reels，翻来翻去全部都是静态图，剪不出东西。

问题不是图不美，是静态图在 Reels 和 TikTok 上撑不过两秒。观众手指一滑就走，你的图再美也没人看完。

但这些图其实是可以动起来的。丢进 Kling AI，加一段 prompt，它会帮你把镜头推进空间、加人走动、加光影和环境的细节。同一张图，变成一支看起来像发展商官方广告的短视频。

四个步骤（真的只有四步） // HOW TO

1

开 Kling，选 Generate

KLING AI

Kling AI 是做视频生成的工具，可以把一张静态图变成一段有镜头运动的视频。进去之后照这个路径走：

Kling→ AI Videos→ Image to Video→ Generate
!

小提醒：不同版本的介面写法会有一点点不一样，有些直接叫 Image to Video，有些在 Generate 里面才让你选。看到「上传图片」的那个框就对了，别找错去 Text to Video。

2

Upload 发展商的概念图

UPLOAD

直接把发展商发你的原图丢进去。用原图，不要用被 WhatsApp 压过很多次的那张，画质越干净，生成出来越稳。

示范图像这种空间感强、看得到前景中景背景的图，动起来效果最好。
✓

哪些图最好用：Lobby、Sky Garden、Pool Deck、Gym、Clubhouse 这种有纵深的空间图，还有外观的黄昏 shot。反过来，太窄的角度、单一家具的 close-up、或者已经贴满字的宣传图，动起来比较容易翻车。

3

整段 Prompt copy paste 进去

PROMPT

下面那段 prompt 整段丢进 prompt 框，不用改，不用翻译。这段是写来锁住原本设计、同时让镜头和人物自然动起来的。

4

点击生成，等它跑完

DONE

按下去之后就让它跑。跑完 download 下来，done ✅ 直接丢进 CapCut 或者你惯用的剪辑软件当开场就可以了。

万能 Prompt，按一下 copy // THE PROMPT

整段贴进 Kling 的 prompt 框。每一张新的概念图都用同一段，不用重写。

PROMPT · 概念图转动态视频
Transform this static real estate concept image into a lively, cinematic property advertisement.

Preserve the original architecture, interior or exterior design, furniture, facilities, landscape, materials, lighting, structural details, and overall visual identity of the project. Do not redesign or alter the original concept.

Create an immersive cinematic camera journey that naturally explores the space. The camera may move forward, gently turn, rise or lower, pass through foreground elements, and create realistic parallax between foreground, midground, and background. Let the camera movement adapt naturally to the composition instead of using a simple zoom or basic pan.

Bring the scene to life with realistic human activity where appropriate. Add a small number of natural-looking people that fit the environment and scale of the scene. They may casually walk through the space, enter or leave the frame, sit, relax, interact, talk, or use the facilities naturally depending on the setting. People should feel like part of the environment, not posed for the camera.

Also add natural environmental movement where appropriate, such as flowing water, moving reflections, gently swaying plants, curtains, subtle lighting changes, passing vehicles, atmospheric movement, or other realistic details that suit the scene.

Use foreground movement and occasional people passing closer to the camera to create stronger depth and a more immersive sense of space.

The overall movement should feel dynamic, fluid, premium, and professionally filmed, like a high-end real estate commercial.

Keep all architecture and major design elements consistent throughout the video. Avoid distorted faces or bodies, duplicated people, unnatural walking, object morphing, disappearing objects, structural changes, sudden camera movement, or unrealistic animation.

框里可以往下滑看完整内容。看起来很长是正常的，越具体，生成出来越不会乱跑。

✓

用法提醒：同一张图可以跑几次，每次镜头走位都不太一样，挑最顺的那条来用。如果生成出来结构变形或者人走得很怪，通常重跑一次就正常了，不是 prompt 有问题。另外记得，这是概念图的动态演绎，不是实景，post 出去的时候讲清楚，客户才不会有误会。
